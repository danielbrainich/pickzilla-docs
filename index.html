Pickzilla

Generate pick lists from your orders in seconds. Every SKU, quantity, and weight in one clean list.

Pickzilla aggregates order line items by SKU into a clean pick list. Select orders from the Orders page, review combined quantities, and save, print, or download as CSV. Saved pick lists are stored with a timestamp and order count for reference. Products with weights display per-SKU totals in g, kg, oz, or lb. No setup required. Works with any catalog including variants.

  • Generate pick lists from a range of orders in one click
  • Items aggregated by SKU with combined quantities
  • Save, print, or download pick lists as CSV
  • Display weights per SKU in g, kg, oz, or lb
  • Review pick list history with timestamps and order counts

Overview

Pickzilla ("the App") is built and operated by Daniel Brainich. This privacy policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

When you install Pickzilla, we access the following data from your Shopify store:

  • Order data (order IDs, line items, quantities, product weights)
  • Product data (SKUs, variant information)

We do not collect personal customer data beyond what is necessary to generate pick lists.

How We Use Your Data

Order and product data is used solely to generate and store pick lists within the app. Pick list data is stored securely and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Data Retention

Pick lists are stored until you delete them or until your store uninstalls the app, at which point your data is deleted.
